Our mission

Revenue clarity for every B2B team

We believe that when sales, marketing, and operations share the same data and the same goals, businesses grow faster and more sustainably. That's the world we're building.

Fyzer was founded in 2019 by a team of former revenue operations leaders who had spent years wrestling with disconnected CRMs, unreliable data, and reports that never quite told the full story.

Today, we serve over 2,400 companies across 14 countries, from early-stage startups to established enterprises with hundreds of salespeople.

Our values

What we stand for

🔍
Radical transparency
We build tools that surface truth, not vanity metrics. Our customers deserve data they can act on, not data that looks good in a slide deck.
🤝
Customer obsession
Every feature we ship starts with a real customer problem. We don't build what's technically interesting — we build what drives revenue outcomes.
⚡
Move with urgency
Revenue doesn't wait. We ship fast, iterate faster, and hold ourselves accountable to the same standards we hold our platform to.
🌍
Inclusive by design
We build for diverse teams and hire diverse talent. Fyzer works for every timezone, every workflow, and every way of doing business.
📐
Precision over noise
One accurate number beats a dashboard full of misleading ones. We obsess over data quality so you don't have to question your reports.
🌱
Long-term thinking
We build for the next decade, not the next quarter. That means durable architecture, sustainable practices, and honest pricing.
