Frequently asked questions

Can I switch plans after signing up?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time from your account settings. Upgrades take effect immediately; downgrades apply at your next billing cycle.
How does the migration from another CRM work?
Every Fyzer plan includes guided data migration. Our team helps you export from your existing CRM, clean and map your data, and import it into Fyzer — typically completed within 3–5 business days.
What integrations are included?
Starter includes Google Ads, LinkedIn, Gmail, and Slack. Growth adds Salesforce, NetSuite, Celigo, HubSpot, Zoom, and 20+ more. Enterprise includes custom API integrations.
Is there a minimum contract length?
Monthly plans have no minimum commitment. Annual plans are billed upfront for a 12-month term. Enterprise contracts are custom and typically 12–24 months.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
You can export all your data at any time, including after cancellation. We retain your data for 30 days post-cancellation, after which it is permanently deleted per our data retention policy.
