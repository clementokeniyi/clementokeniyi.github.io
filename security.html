Security & compliance

Enterprise-grade security, by default

Your revenue data is among the most sensitive in your business. Fyzer was built from day one with security, privacy, and compliance as core requirements — not afterthoughts.

Infrastructure

Built on world-class cloud infrastructure

Fyzer runs on AWS with multi-region redundancy, automatic failover, and continuous availability monitoring. Our infrastructure team monitors platform health 24/7.

  • 99.9% uptime SLA with financial backing
  • Multi-region deployment (US, EU, APAC)
  • Automated failover and disaster recovery
  • Real-time status page at status.fyzerco.com
  • Data encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3)

Access control

Granular permissions for every user and team

Fyzer's role-based access control lets admins define exactly what each user can see, edit, and export — at the object, field, and record level. Supports SSO via SAML 2.0 and OAuth 2.0.

  • Role-based access control (RBAC)
  • SSO via SAML 2.0 (Okta, Azure AD, Google)
  • Two-factor authentication enforced per team
  • Audit logs for every user action
  • IP allowlisting and session management

Data privacy

Your data is yours

📤
Data portability
Export all your data at any time in standard formats (CSV, JSON). No lock-in, no barriers.
🗑
Right to deletion
Delete any record or your entire account instantly. Data is permanently purged within 30 days per our retention policy.
🏠
Data residency
Choose where your data lives — US, EU (Frankfurt), or APAC (Singapore). Residency is guaranteed and auditable.
🔍
Audit logging
Every action taken in Fyzer is logged — who did what, when, from where. Exportable for compliance review.
🚫
No data selling
We never sell, share, or use your CRM data to train third-party models. Your data is used only to run your instance of Fyzer.
📋
DPA available
Data Processing Agreements are available for all plans. Enterprise customers receive a customised DPA as standard.
